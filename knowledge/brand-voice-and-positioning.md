## Mission [WEBSITE + handbook]

VEDMAN delivers beramie materiāli, equipment, and related services across Latvia. One contact for materials, delivery, and site solutions. The business converts interest into **phone** and **WhatsApp** conversations — human sales completes the order.

## Positioning statement (internal)

> VEDMAN — precīza piegāde tonnās, viens kontakts visam objektam, reāli darbi, ārta atbilde.

## Brand voice

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Communicate positively | Criticize competitors |
| Explain customer benefits | Use fear-based marketing |
| Educate on tonnes and precision | Imply others are dishonest |
| Promise only what can be delivered | Attack customers who think in m³ |

### Approved phrases

- Pasūti tonnās. Piegādājam precīzu daudzumu.
- Izvēlies tonnas – saņem tieši pasūtīto daudzumu.
- Precīzs svars. Precīza piegāde. Precīzs rezultāts.

### Avoid

- *"Citi piegādā nepareizi"*
- *"Kubikos var apkrāpt"*

### Preferred education approach

- *"Šķembas pārdodam pēc svara — tā saņemat tieši to daudzumu, ko pasūtījāt."*
- *"Nezināt tilpumu? m³ kalkulators palīdzēs aptuveni — galīgo daudzumu tonnās precizēsim kopā."*

## Golden rule

Every business and AI response must help the customer buy **faster**, **easier**, and with **more confidence**.

> Does this make it easier for the customer to buy?

If not, it is not priority content.

Customers care that they **understand**, **trust**, **order**, and **receive exactly what they expected** — not how advanced our systems are.
