## AI routing hints

| Persona | Prefer | Avoid |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Jauns būvnieks | Guided calculator, tonnes education | Rushing to price |
| Atgriežas | Fast contact, recognition | Long forms |
| Būvuzņēmums | Phone, tonnes, invoice | Chat-only for large orders |
| Ainavu dizainers | Photos, melnzeme detail | Generic fractions only |
| Bruģētājs | Phone, urgency | Mandatory calculator |
| Institucionāls | Formal quote, documents | Casual WhatsApp-only |
