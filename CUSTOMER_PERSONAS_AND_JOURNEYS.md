# Customer Personas & Journeys — VEDMAN

**Document type:** UX research & conversion psychology  
**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Status:** Planning — no code, no implementation  
**Related:** `MILESTONE3_CONVERSION_SYSTEM.md`, `VEDMAN_DEVELOPMENT_HANDBOOK.md`

**Goal:** Understand how **different** customers buy beramie materiāli and related services in Latvia — not one generic buyer, but six distinct decision-makers with different fears, channels, and success criteria.

**North Star:** Does this make it easier for the customer to buy?

---

## Executive summary

VEDMAN serves a **split market**: anxious first-time homeowners, fast repeat buyers, volume-driven construction firms, aesthetic-driven landscapers, deadline-driven pavers, and process-driven institutional buyers. They do not share one CTA, one unit of measure framing, or one communication preference.

The homepage must feel like **six doors into one trusted supplier** — not six different websites. Conversion wins when each persona finds their answer in under 30 seconds and contacts VEDMAN in their preferred channel without feeling stupid, rushed, or ignored.

# Persona 1 — Private Homeowner (Building a House)

*Andris, 38, builds family house in Pierīga. First construction project. Wife handles budget. He Googles at night on phone.*

### Goals

- Get the **right** material for foundation, drainage, driveway, yard
- Avoid expensive mistakes (wrong fraction, wrong amount)
- Feel guided — not judged for not knowing terms
- Receive clear price and delivery date before committing
- One supplier for several needs (šķembas, smilts, melnzeme, maybe manipulator)

### Fears

- Ordering wrong fraction (*"0-32 vai 0-45 — kas man vajag?"*)
- Ordering wrong quantity (*"Vai pietiks? Vai pārpalikums?"*)
- Hidden costs (delivery, minimum order, weekend surcharge)
- Truck cannot access property (*"Šaurs vārtu caurums"*)
- Supplier disappears after payment or quote
- Being sold m³ when industry bills tonnes — surprise at delivery

### Questions (typical)

- Kas man vajag pamatiem / drenāžai / piebraucamajam ceļam?
- Cik tonnu vajag aptuveni?
- Vai piegādājat šaurā vietā / pāri žogam?
- Cik maksā piegāde līdz manai adresē?
- Vai varu maksāt ar pārskaitījumu?
- Kad varat piegādāt?

### Buying behaviour

- **Research-heavy** — reads Google, SS.lv, asks neighbours
- **Slow decision** — days to weeks unless contractor pushes
- **High anxiety** — needs reassurance more than speed
- Often orders in **phases** (foundation first, landscaping later)
- May involve spouse — decision not solo

### Preferred communication

1. **WhatsApp** — feels less intimidating than phone; can send address pin, photos
2. **Phone** — when anxious or urgent; wants human voice
3. Rarely email

### Preferred CTA

- **Primary:** *Uzzini cenu* with guided help — *"Nezinu ko izvēlēties — palīdziet"*
- **Secondary:** WhatsApp with pre-filled context
- Phone visible as safety net

### Homepage expectations

- Plain language: *"Pamatiem"*, *"Ceļam"*, *"Dārzam"* — not only fraction codes
- Real photos of house projects, not abstract graphics
- Clear geography: *"Piegādājam Pierīgā, Rīgā, visā Latvijā"*
- 3-step process visible early
- No assumption they know tonnes vs m³

### Calculator expectations

- **Guided wizard:** *"Ko būvējat?"* → dimensions → approximate m³ → *"Parasti pasūta X–Y tonnas — precizēsim"*
- Must feel like help, not exam
- Output should feed quote automatically

### Quote expectations

- Short, not overwhelming — 2 steps maximum
- Material dropdown with **friendly grouping**
- Tonnes introduced gently with education
- Add-ons visible: *šaura vieta*, *pāri žogam*, *vajag konsultāciju*
- Confirmation: *"Atbildēsim šodien"*

### After-sale expectations

- Proactive message: delivery window, driver contact
- Willingness to adjust if amount slightly off (human relationship)
- Same contact person if possible — builds trust for phase 2 orders

# Persona 2 — Returning Private Customer

*Līga, 45, ordered melnzeme and grants twice last year for property in Ogre. Knows quality. Busy.*

### Goals

- Reorder **fast** — same or similar as before
- Skip education — she already trusts VEDMAN
- Confirm price hasn't changed wildly
- Same-day or next-day delivery if possible

### Fears

- Website changed and ordering got harder
- Having to re-explain her property constraints
- Slower response than last time
- New person on phone who doesn't know her

### Questions

- Vai tāda pati cena kā pagājušoreiz?
- Var rīt piegādāt?
- Vēl 8 tonnas tā paša grants?
- Tas pats šoferis / tāda pati piegādes vieta?

### Buying behaviour

- **Low research** — may not scroll homepage at all
- **Direct action** — phone or WhatsApp from saved contact
- Website visit only if checking number or sending link to neighbour
- High lifetime value if retained

### Preferred communication

1. **WhatsApp** — async, copy-paste previous order style
2. **Phone** — one call, done

### Preferred CTA

- **Primary:** Click-to-call or WhatsApp — **not** long form
- Optional: *"Atkārtot pasūtījumu"* concept (future CRM)
- Minimal friction — every extra click hurts conversion

### Homepage expectations

- Phone number **immediately** visible
- Recognizable brand — *"Jā, tas ir viņi"*
- No need for long hero — speed to contact
- Hours visible — *"Can I call now?"*

### Calculator expectations

- **Does not need it** — skip or hide for returning users (future)
- If shown, one-tap dismiss

### Quote expectations

- Remembers fields — ideally pre-filled from history (future)
- Today: short message template she already knows
- Send → WhatsApp in under 60 seconds

### After-sale expectations

- Consistency — same quality, same reliability
- Being recognized: *"Jā, atceramies Jūsu adresi"*
- Priority treatment without asking — loyalty reward

# Persona 3 — Construction Company

*SIA "Būveks", foreman Māris orders weekly for active sites in Rīga. Three projects running.*

### Goals

- Reliable supply — truck shows up, correct fraction, correct tonnes
- **Fast quote** — needs number today for client estimate
- Invoice / PVN documentation
- Multiple deliveries per week to different addresses
- Backup supplier relationship when primary quarry busy

### Fears

- No-show or late truck — site stops, workers idle
- Wrong fraction delivered — rework cost
- Price drift without notice
- Cannot reach anyone after 16:00
- Amateur operator — no invoice, no scales

### Questions

- Cik tonnu minimāli?
- Cena par tonnu ar piegādi uz [adrese]?
- Vai varat rīt no rīta?
- Rēķins ar PVN?
- Vai ir pašizgāzējs 20t+?
- Kas ir kontaktpersona piegādēm?

### Buying behaviour

- **Phone-first** — foreman calls while driving
- Repeat orders — relationship > website
- Website used to **verify legitimacy** for new management / accounting
- Decisions in minutes, not days
- Volume — high revenue per customer

### Preferred communication

1. **Phone** — immediate
2. **WhatsApp** — send site address pin, photos, tonnage list
3. Email for invoices — off-site

### Preferred CTA

- **Primary:** Prominent **phone** in header
- **Secondary:** WhatsApp for multi-line orders
- Quote form useful when office staff ordering, not foreman on site

### Homepage expectations

- Professional, not flashy
- Material fractions clearly listed — speaks his language
- Payment: *Ar PVN / Bez PVN*, bankas pārskaitījums
- Company name visible — SIA, not anonymous
- Gallery of commercial-scale deliveries

### Calculator expectations

- **Irrelevant** — he knows tonnes
- Do not force wizard — respect expertise

### Quote expectations

- Structured WhatsApp OK if includes: material, fraction, tonnes, address, time window
- No childish UX — efficient fields
- Multiple lines in comment field — normal for him

### After-sale expectations

- On-time delivery window communicated
- Problem resolution fast — one call fixes it
- Monthly relationship — predictable pricing where possible
- Credit terms (if offered) — major loyalty driver

# Persona 4 — Landscaper

*Dace, landscape designer, orders melnzeme, smilts, decorative stone for client gardens in Rīga and Pierīga.*

### Goals

- Materials that **look right** on site — colour, cleanliness, sieve quality
- Reliable timing — client visit scheduled
- Right melnzeme type (sijāta vs nesijāta)
- Sometimes decorative fractions (oļi, granīta šķembas)
- Professional image in front of her client

### Fears

- Dirty or wrong-grade soil visible to client
- Late delivery — she stands embarrassed at empty plot
- Cannot describe fraction — orders wrong decorative stone
- Supplier doesn't understand aesthetic requirements
- Truck damages client driveway

### Questions

- Vai melnzeme ir sijāta, bez zariem?
- Kā izskatās grants / oļi? (wants photos)
- Vai var piegādāt sestdien?
- Vai var izlīdzināt?
- Cik tonnu uz 50 m² dārzu?

### Buying behaviour

- **Visual decision-maker** — gallery and photos matter enormously
- **WhatsApp-heavy** — sends client garden photos
- Plans ahead but client changes cause urgency
- Medium order sizes — recurring seasonal peaks (spring/fall)
- Refers VEDMAN to clients if experience good

### Preferred communication

1. **WhatsApp** — photos, pins, voice notes
2. **Phone** — when timing critical

### Preferred CTA

- **Primary:** WhatsApp with photo attach prompt
- Material cards with **real texture photos** — not CSS gradients
- *Uzzini cenu* with melnzeme / smilts pre-paths

### Homepage expectations

- Gallery dominated by **finished gardens, soil, stone**
- Melnzeme and smilts prominent
- Service: *izlīdzināt*, *teritorijas sakopšana*
- Seasonal messaging opportunity — *"Pavasara apstādījumi"*

### Calculator expectations

- Area-based helper: *"Dārza platība m²"* → approximate tonnes melnzeme
- Must stress **approximate** — she adjusts with experience
- Visual examples: *"10 cm kārta uz 100 m²"*

### Quote expectations

- Comment field important — aesthetic notes
- Add-on: *izlīdzināt*, *vajag konsultāciju*
- Image-friendly WhatsApp handoff

### After-sale expectations

- Clean delivery — no mud on client street
- Willingness to swap if material visibly wrong
- Becomes referral channel to homeowners (Persona 1)

# Persona 5 — Paving Contractor

*Edgars, small paving crew, orders 0-32 / 0-45 šķembas weekly. Knows exactly what he needs.*

### Goals

- **Correct fraction** — 0-32 or 0-45, no substitutes
- **ASAP delivery** — crew scheduled, weather window
- Right tonnes on truck — one shot, no under-delivery
- Access for truck — knows if pašizgāzējs fits
- Price stable enough to quote his clients

### Fears

- Late truck — crew costs money idle
- Wrong fraction — job stops
- Under-tonnage — second trip delays project
- Supplier argues m³ vs tonnes at delivery
- Cannot get through on phone Friday afternoon

### Questions

- Vai ir 0-32 šodien?
- 15 tonnas, Ādažos, kad varat?
- Cena par tonnu ar piegādi?
- Mašīna 20t vai mazāka?
- Var steidzami?

### Buying behaviour

- **Extremely phone-first** — calls, doesn't browse
- **Zero tolerance** for friction — won't read hero copy
- Website = phone number verification + maybe fractions list
- Orders same materials repeatedly
- Weather and season drive spikes

### Preferred communication

1. **Phone** — always
2. WhatsApp only for address pin when driving

### Preferred CTA

- **Primary:** `tel:22312828` — largest tap target on mobile
- Quote form **secondary** — for office hours planning only
- *Steidzami* add-on must be one tap in quote

### Homepage expectations

- Phone above fold, always
- Fraction chips 0-32, 0-45, 0-56 visible instantly
- Hours + *"Zvaniet — atbildam ātri"*

### Calculator expectations

- **Does not use** — may annoy him if forced
- Hide behind optional link only

### Quote expectations

- Minimal fields: material, fraction, tonnes, address, steidzami
- Pre-filled WhatsApp — send in 20 seconds
- Or skip entirely — he calls

### After-sale expectations

- Truck on time or proactive delay call
- Exact tonnes — weighbridge trust
- Repeat weekly — fastest supplier wins

# Persona 6 — Municipality / Farm / Industrial

*Pagasta pārvalde or large farm — orders for road maintenance, parking, drainage, industrial yard. Procurement rules.*

### Goals

- **Documented supplier** — invoice, contract readiness
- Large volume planning — weeks ahead
- Reliability over price margin — political/operational risk if fail
- Multiple delivery points
- Transparent specification (fraction, tonnes, delivery schedule)

### Fears

- Supplier too small for obligation
- No proper invoice / PVN
- Delivery failure becomes public problem
- Informal communication — no paper trail
- Price changes mid-project

### Questions

- Vai varat piegādāt 200+ tonnas pa fāzēm?
- Rēķins ar PVN, sadarbības pieredze ar pašvaldībām?
- Kāda ir piegādes garantija?
- Vai varat nodrošināt dokumentāciju?
- Kontaktpersona projektam?

### Buying behaviour

- **Phone + formal email** — tender or direct negotiation
- Long sales cycle — website is credibility check only
- Committee or manager decision — not impulse
- Repeat annual if trusted (roads, yards)

### Preferred communication

1. **Phone** — initial qualification
2. **Email** — quotes, invoices, schedules
3. WhatsApp rare — informal channel may feel unprofessional

### Preferred CTA

- **Primary:** Phone + visible company identity (AMAPU SIA)
- **Secondary:** Contact for commercial / large orders
- Dedicated line or note: *"Lieliem pasūtījumiem zvaniet"*

### Homepage expectations

- Institutional trust — real projects, scale photos
- Payment and PVN clearly stated in footer
- Geography: regional and national coverage
- Services beyond materials — manipulator, zemes darbi
- No gimmicky UX — sober professionalism

### Calculator expectations

- Not used — volume known from project spec
- PDF/spec download (future) more valuable

### Quote expectations

- Formal quote request — may not use WhatsApp modal at all
- Website should route to phone/commercial contact
- If form used: fields for org name, project reference, total tonnes

### After-sale expectations

- Delivery schedule in writing
- Batch documentation — weights, dates
- Account manager continuity
- Problem escalation path — one senior contact

## Journey A — First-Time Visitor

**Profile:** Persona 1 or comparison shopper. No prior VEDMAN knowledge. Arrived via search, link, or curiosity.

### What the customer thinks

| Phase | Internal monologue |
|-------|-------------------|
| Land | *"Vai šie pārdod to, kas man vajag?"* |
| Scan | *"Izskatās normāli… vai arī tikai skaista lapa?"* |
| Trust check | *"Vai ir īsti cilvēki, mašīnas, projekti?"* |
| Order check | *"Kā pasūtīt? Vai būs sarežģīti?"* |
| Geography | *"Vai brauc arī pie manis?"* |
| Decide | *"Zvanīšu, rakstīšu, vai aizeju"* |

### What the customer sees (today)

- Strong brand headline, less explicit material list in H1
- Gradient hero — limited real-world proof
- Material cards with texture placeholders
- Multiple green buttons — choice friction
- Gallery may show empty/technical message
- Quote modal with m³ default

### What the customer needs

- Immediate answer: materials + geography + how to order
- Human-accessible path — phone or WhatsApp
- Permission to not know — guided help
- Proof before commitment

### What could stop the purchase

- No real photos → *"fake company"*
- Empty gallery → *"nobody uses them"*
- Confusing fractions → paralysis
- Too many CTAs → leave to call SS.lv lister
- No response-time promise → delay fear

### How VEDMAN should respond

- Hero: job + geography + one primary CTA
- Trust layer in first screen: photo, phone, hours, tonnes line
- Guided quote — 2 steps, education not interrogation
- Same-day response promise
- WhatsApp message that makes customer feel competent

### What the customer sees

- Full marketing homepage — **more than they need**

- Brand recognition

### What could stop the purchase

- Website slower than calling from history

### How VEDMAN should respond

- Phone/WhatsApp always zero-scroll on mobile
- Future: recognition, repeat order, saved address
- Staff: recognize repeat callers — CRM memory
- Consistency in quality and response time

## Journey D — SS.lv Visitor

**Profile:** Latvian classifieds habit. Used to phone-in-title, price hints, rough photos.

### What the customer thinks

| Phase | Internal monologue |
|-------|-------------------|
| SS.lv | *"OK cena, zvanu… vai skatos mājaslapu"* |
| Website | *"Vai tas pats uzņēmums? Vai nopietnāks?"* |
| Trust | *"SS.lv bija vienkārši — šeit var būt dārgāk vai profesionālāk"* |
| Decide | *"Ja lapa izskatās OK — zvanu šiem"* |

### What the customer sees

- More polished site than typical SS.lv lister
- May lack price — SS.lv had number in title
- Structured quote vs raw phone

### What the customer needs

- Confirmation: same company, same phone
- Proof VEDMAN is **real** — gallery, trucks
- Phone immediately — SS.lv mindset is call now
- Not feel tricked into form instead of human

### What could stop the purchase

- No phone prominent — returns to SS.lv tab
- Over-designed site without prices — *"pārāk dārgi"*
- Form-only contact — distrust

### How VEDMAN should respond

- SS.lv listing and website **same phone, same brand**
- Website proves professionalism SS.lv can't show
- Unique SS.lv text — not copy-paste (handbook §11)
- Price on SS.lv: *"no X"* or *"zvani"* — drive to conversation
- Site reinforces: *"Skaidra cena pēc pieprasījuma"*

## Journey F — Direct Phone Recommendation

**Profile:** Neighbour, contractor, relative said *"zvani VEDMAN"*. Highest trust entry.

### What the customer thinks

| Phase | Internal monologue |
|-------|-------------------|
| Referral | *"Uzticu, jo Jānis teica"* |

### What could stop the purchase

- Answer phone fast — referral ROI is highest
- Ask: *"Kas ieteica?"* — track word-of-mouth

- Website supports referrer sharing link to material section
