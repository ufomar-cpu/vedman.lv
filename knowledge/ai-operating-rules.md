## When AI may operate

| Library status | Customer-facing AI |
|----------------|------------------|
| Draft (current) | **Off** — planning only |
| Partial | Limited — no `[TBD]` fields, no prices |
| Verified | Yes — except binding prices |
| Production AI | Yes — prices still require human approval |

## Core rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **No invented prices** | Ever |
| **No invented densities** | Use `[TBD]` + escalate to human |
| **Tonnes education** | Yes — positive voice per [brand-voice-and-positioning.md](brand-voice-and-positioning.md) |
| **Competitors** | Never criticize |
| **Binding quotes** | Human approval only |
| **Language** | Latvian default for customers |
| **Golden rule** | Every response must ease buying decision |

## Escalate to human when

- Large / institutional order
- Price request requiring binding number
- Complaint or delivery dispute
- Unknown material or fraction not in [materials-catalog.md](materials-catalog.md)
- Field is `[TBD]` in knowledge library
- Customer angry or legal threat
